King Brewing Company

Introduction

King Brewing Co. was formed in 1994, reviving a brewing tradition in southeastern Michigan that had ceased in the early 1950s when the last small brewer was forced to close in Pontiac.

When we opened our doors, we brought back the experience of drinking fresh, locally-crafted beer — something Michiganders hadn't enjoyed in over 40 years. Our goal was simple: make great beer and create a place where people could come together, relax, and enjoy it.

Over the years, we experimented with countless recipes, developed our signature beers, and built a community around craft brewing long before it became a mainstream movement. From our IPAs to our porters, every beer was brewed with passion and care by owner Scott King and our dedicated team.

King Brewing became more than just a brewery — it was a gathering place. A spot where regulars knew each other by name, where live music filled the room on weekends, and where the beer was always fresh off the tap.

After almost 15 years of fighting the good fight, we eventually closed our doors. But the memories, the recipes, and the community we built will live on forever.

— The King Brewing Team
