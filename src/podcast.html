King Brewing Podcast

Listen to the brewers, owners, employees, and regulars talk about King Brewing.

Brewer Scott King

Sunday, July 30, 2006

In this episode, brewer and owner Scott King sits down to talk about the beers, what's going on at King Brewing, and what it means to be a craft brewer in southeastern Michigan.

Scott shares the story of how King Brewing got started in 1994, the challenges of running a small brewpub, and the philosophy behind the recipes that made King Brewing a beloved local institution.

Whether you're a longtime regular or new to craft beer, this is a great listen to hear the passion and heart behind every King Brewing pint.
