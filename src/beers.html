Our Beers

A selection of King Brewing's finest craft beers — brewed with passion in Pontiac, Michigan.

King IPA

Bold and hoppy, our IPA is the flagship of the King Brewing lineup. Packed with citrus and pine flavors, it's the perfect beer for hop lovers.

Pale Ale

Smooth and balanced, our Pale Ale is a crowd favorite. Light golden color with a subtle malt sweetness and a clean hoppy finish.

Irish Red

A classic Irish-style Red Ale with a rich amber color. Smooth and malty with a hint of caramel and a balanced finish.

Superior Porter

Rich and full-bodied, with notes of chocolate and coffee. A dark beauty that warms you from the inside out — perfect for a Michigan winter.

Mocha Java Stout

The ultimate coffee lover's beer. Brewed with real coffee and chocolate, this stout delivers layers of mocha and java in every sip.

Bertolini's Crown-Brown Ale

A special brown ale with complex malty flavors. Named in honor of a local favorite, this ale delivers nutty sweetness with a smooth finish.

Two Fisted Old Ale

Strong and complex, Two Fisted is King Brewing's big beer. Rich malt character with dried fruit notes — best enjoyed slowly and savored.

Hefeweissen

A classic German-style wheat beer. Light, hazy, and refreshing with banana and clove notes. The perfect summer sipper.

Doppel Bock

A rich, malty German-style strong lager. Deep amber color with sweet caramel and toasted bread flavors. A seasonal favorite.

Festbier

Brewed for Oktoberfest celebrations. A smooth, golden lager with a bready malt sweetness — the traditional way Germans kick off the festival.

Continental Lager

Crisp and refreshing, our Continental Lager is clean and light with a subtle hop presence. Perfect for any occasion and any season.
